# Disclaimer

**LLM is a tool for running a language model you supply. It ships with no model.** What the app
writes is produced by that file, on your device, and is not a statement by eVersor-HN.

## Output

A language model produces plausible text, not verified fact. It will state wrong things with
complete confidence, invent sources, and get arithmetic, law, medicine, finance and safety-critical
detail wrong. **Do not act on its output in any matter that carries real consequence without
independent verification.** It is not professional advice of any kind.

## Content controls

The app lets you shape the assistant's behaviour, up to and including presets that impose no
restraint, and the story mode offers an explicit-content switch. These are requests made of the
model: what a given model file actually does with them is a property of that file, not of this app.
The app applies no content filter of its own and makes no representation about what any model will
write. **You choose the model and the settings, and you are responsible for what you generate and
for what you do with it.** For adults only.

## Model files

Model files come from third parties under their own licenses and terms, which are between you and
whoever published them. Verify what you download. A model file determines what the application
computes; treat one from an untrusted source as you would any untrusted executable.

## Performance

Speed, memory use and whether a given model runs at all depend on your device, the model file and
its format. Figures shown in the app are measurements and estimates for your device at that moment,
not guarantees.
